Core palette

The aim is clinical trust without the cold, sterile feel that healthcare sites often have. Soft neutrals do most of the work, and one accent color carries the actions.

Role	Name	Hex	Use
Primary	Deep Teal	
#0F5E64	Headers, primary buttons, key icons
Accent	Fresh Mint	
#3CC4A8	Highlights, success states, small badges
Background	Warm Paper	
#FAFAF7	Main page background
Surface	Pure White	
#FFFFFF	Cards, panels, modals
Text	Ink Slate	
#1E2A2E	Body and headings
Muted text	Stone Gray	
#6B7A7E	Captions, helper text, metadata
Border	Mist	
#E4EAE8	Dividers, card outlines, inputs
Soft tint	Sage Wash	
#EEF6F3	Hover states, info panels, selected rows
Alert	Coral Soft	
#E9735E	Errors and urgent notices (use sparingly)
